Privacy Policy — Stuck Arrows

Effective date: 8 August 2026

This policy explains what information the Stuck Arrows mobile game (“the app”, “we”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Stuck Arrows is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by ads.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location, and the app has no accounts or sign-in. The app collects:

  • Gameplay and usage analytics. Events such as levels started and completed, coins earned and spent, and ads shown, sent to Google Firebase Analytics so we can measure retention and improve the game. Firebase generates a random app instance identifier for your installation — it is not linked to your real identity, and it is reset if you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.
  • Device and app information. Along with those events, Firebase Analytics records standard technical details such as your device model, operating system version, app version, language and country. Country is derived from your IP address; we do not collect precise or GPS location.
  • Advertising identifier. Our ads provider (Google AdMob) may access your device’s advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings. On iOS the app asks for your permission first, and declining means the identifier is not used.
  • Crash diagnostics. If the app crashes or hits an error, Google Firebase Crashlytics records the crash — the stack trace, the device model and operating system version, and the app version — so we can fix it. These reports are tied to a random installation identifier, not to you.

Your game progress — levels, stars, coins, power-ups and settings — is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us.

The app is fully playable offline; the data above is only sent when you have a network connection.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve gameplay, difficulty, and the in-game economy.
  • To decide which features and levels to build next.
  • To display ads and, where applicable, personalized ads.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

These services process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (analytics, crash diagnostics, feature configuration) — policies.google.com/privacy, firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) to limit ad personalization. On iOS, decline the tracking prompt or turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Analytics: you can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the app, which also clears the app instance identifier described above.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Analytics data tied to your random app instance identifier is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above, in line with Google Firebase’s retention settings. To request deletion of data associated with your app instance identifier, contact us at itinyjava+stuckarrows@gmail.com and we will action the request.

Children’s privacy

Stuck Arrows is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date above.

Contact
